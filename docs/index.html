About

Models by thejoinery (CC BY 4.0, one CC BY-SA 4.0), read from the public Sketchfab viewer, split into timbers, re-lit with a baked paper-sunrise look and given an assembly animation. The animations were checked for timbers passing through each other, and some meshes were corrected where the author's pose was not the true closed joint.

Four joints with no licensed source model — 台持ち継ぎ, 雇い枘仕口, 三方差し仕口 and 二方差し仕口 — are authored for this collection from the reference sheet 柱（軸組）の納まり and released CC BY 4.0.
